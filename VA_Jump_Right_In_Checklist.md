# VA Jump Right In Checklist

## Purpose Of The Role

The new VA should help the team create deal flow, organize leads, perform first-pass research, and keep opportunities moving until the team can decide whether to wholesale, list, assign, double close, develop, or pass.

The role is still being shaped, so the first version should be treated as a 2 to 4 week operating trial with clear reporting and weekly review.

## Start Here: First Day Checklist

- Sign contractor agreement before any paid work begins.
- Confirm pay rate, weekly hours, payment schedule, bonus structure, and whether any advance is being offered.
- Confirm the trial period and review cadence.
- Add VA to the correct communication channels.
- Keep internal decision-making chats separate from the VA-facing group chat.
- Give VA access only to approved tools and accounts.
- Walk through the business model:
  - residential wholesale and flip opportunities;
  - vacant land opportunities;
  - retail listing opportunities that should not be forced into an investor deal;
  - development or builder-facing land opportunities.
- Explain the team's ethics standard:
  - disclose when the team is acting as investor versus agent;
  - do not pressure distressed sellers;
  - flag situations where listing may serve the seller better than an investor offer;
  - escalate estate, probate, disabled-family-member, elderly-owner, or hardship situations before negotiating.
- Review the current target markets and who covers each area.
- Review how the team votes on deals, expenses, new members, and tool decisions.
- Set the required reporting format for each day.

## First Week Checklist

- Complete live walkthrough of the lead source platform selected for the trial.
- Build the first lead list based on the team-approved target criteria.
- Send or prepare the first SMS/mail campaign, if approved.
- Set up lead tracking with statuses.
- Create a simple lead handoff form or tracker.
- Define what counts as a warm lead.
- Define when VA can continue the conversation and when she must escalate.
- Have VA submit a daily activity report each workday.
- Have VA present at least one sample opportunity to the team using the agreed deal summary format.
- Hold a weekly review after the first week to decide whether the hours, duties, and tools are working.

## Likely Core Duties

### Lead Sourcing And List Building

- Pull property lists based on target area, property type, acreage, ownership status, distress signals, and deal strategy.
- Clean lead lists before outreach.
- Segment leads by category:
  - vacant land;
  - infill lots;
  - large acreage;
  - residential flip candidates;
  - pre-foreclosure or distressed residential;
  - FSBO or expired listing;
  - probate/estate;
  - potential retail listing.
- Prepare lists for SMS, mail, or calling campaigns.
- Track source, date pulled, platform, campaign type, and assigned owner.

### Outreach Support

- Run SMS outreach if the team chooses an SMS-first strategy.
- Prepare mailer lists and blind offer campaigns if mail is approved.
- Respond to interested sellers.
- Answer inbound calls from SMS or mail campaigns.
- Ask pre-qualification questions.
- Avoid full-day cold calling unless the team changes the role.
- If cold calling is required, clarify whether this VA will do it or whether a separate caller is needed.

### Seller Pre-Qualification

Collect and document:

- property address or parcel number;
- owner name and best callback number;
- seller motivation;
- asking price;
- timeline to sell;
- mortgage, lien, estate, probate, or ownership issues;
- property condition;
- access, road frontage, and visible issues;
- utilities;
- survey status;
- wetlands, flood zone, slope, restrictions, or easements;
- structures on site;
- whether seller wants investor cash offer, listing help, or just information.

### Land Due Diligence

- Review property in the selected land platform.
- Check acreage, zoning, parcel boundaries, slope, flood zone/FEMA, wetlands, road access, buildability, and utilities.
- Pull comparable sales and price-per-acre comps.
- Compare platform pricing against manual comps.
- Call county offices when platform information needs verification.
- Ask county about subdivision rules, setbacks, minimum lot size, frontage, utility requirements, and permitting.
- Check whether parcel may work for infill, subdivision, builder resale, assignment, double close, or long-term development.
- Prepare a concept summary for promising parcels.

### Residential Due Diligence

- Pull basic property facts and ownership.
- Review estimated value and recent comps.
- Check mortgage, foreclosure, probate, estate, tax, or title red flags where visible.
- Identify whether the opportunity is more likely:
  - wholesale;
  - flip;
  - retail listing;
  - referral/listing lead;
  - not a fit.
- Flag properties where seller equity and circumstances suggest a retail listing may be more ethical or profitable.

### Deal Presentation

For every potential deal, VA should submit a short deal brief:

- property address;
- property type;
- seller contact and notes;
- source of lead;
- seller asking price;
- estimated value;
- estimated offer range;
- key risks;
- recommended strategy;
- needed team decision;
- next follow-up date;
- assigned team member.

### Transaction And Follow-Up Support

- Prepare contract drafts only after team approval.
- Send contracts for signature using the approved process.
- Walk sellers through basic contract questions within approved script.
- Escalate legal, title, estate, probate, assignment, double-close, or hard negotiation questions.
- Coordinate with title company or attorney once a deal is approved.
- Track contract deadlines.
- Keep seller updated.
- Track buyer, builder, investor, realtor, or listing follow-up.

### Disposition And Marketing Support

- Build a buyers/builders list for approved deals.
- Research builders and developers in the property area.
- Prepare marketing summaries for approved properties.
- Coordinate drone photos, property photos, maps, and listing materials when needed.
- Post or coordinate posts on approved platforms only.
- Track buyer interest and showing requests.
- Support flat-fee MLS, Zillow, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, land sites, InvestorLift, BuyerBridge, or other disposition tools if the team chooses them.

### Admin And Accountability

- Maintain the lead tracker daily.
- Maintain a task inbox for deals needing votes or review.
- Follow up with team members when a decision is needed.
- Schedule calls when needed.
- Keep meeting notes and action items.
- Send end-of-day report.
- Send weekly pipeline report.
- Track tool issues, missing access, and process gaps.

## Daily Report Template

Each workday, VA should send:

- hours worked;
- platforms used;
- leads pulled;
- messages sent;
- seller replies;
- inbound calls;
- warm leads created;
- deals researched;
- contracts or offers prepared;
- follow-ups completed;
- blockers;
- decisions needed from team;
- plan for next workday.

## Weekly Pipeline Report Template

- total leads pulled;
- total outreach sent;
- response rate;
- warm leads;
- offers made;
- contracts sent;
- contracts signed;
- listings or retail opportunities created;
- deals passed and why;
- tool issues;
- recommended changes for next week.

## Decisions The Team Still Needs To Make

### Role Scope

- Is this VA focused on land, residential, or both?
- Is the first priority quick revenue from residential wholesale/listing opportunities or longer-term land/development opportunities?
- Is she expected to cold call, or only handle SMS, mail responses, inbound calls, and warm follow-up?
- If cold calling is needed, will the team hire a separate caller?
- Can team members use unused VA time for their own related tasks, or is all time reserved for collective deal flow?

### Hours And Pay

- Confirm weekly hours: 20, 30, or another number.
- Confirm whether hours are fixed daily or flexible weekly.
- Confirm whether 30 hours means 6 hours per day, 5 days per week.
- Confirm pay rate.
- Confirm payment cadence: weekly at first, then biweekly, or biweekly from the start.
- Decide whether an advance is allowed.
- If an advance is allowed, confirm whether it is deducted from first pay.
- Confirm deal bonus amount and when it is paid.
- Confirm whether there is a 30-day review, 90-day review, or both.

### Tools

- Choose trial platforms:
  - Land Insight;
  - Land Portal;
  - PropertyRadar;
  - Mojo;
  - CallTools;
  - RingCentral;
  - InvestorLift;
  - BuyerBridge;
  - land investor association/community access.
- Confirm which tool handles SMS blast.
- Confirm whether SMS compliance/registration is required before campaigns begin.
- Confirm whether the chosen tool can support both residential and land.
- Confirm who pays for tool seats.
- Confirm whether Chase or another partner is splitting platform costs.
- Confirm whether VA gets individual credentials or shared limited access.

### Lead Handoff

- What exactly makes a lead "warm"?
- What information must be captured before a lead moves to team review?
- Who reviews land leads?
- Who reviews residential leads?
- Who handles negotiation?
- Who signs off before an offer is made?
- Who sends contracts?
- Who is the seller-facing point of contact after contract?

### Deal Strategy

- When do you wholesale?
- When do you assign?
- When do you double close?
- When do you list retail instead of making an investor offer?
- When do you pursue development?
- When do you pass?
- What is the minimum expected profit for each type of deal?
- What offer formula should VA use for land?
- What offer formula should VA use for residential?

### Retail Listings

- Which agents cover which areas?
- How are listing opportunities assigned?
- What happens when multiple team agents are willing to take the listing?
- Does any portion of a listing commission go back into the collective?
- How will the team handle taxes and legal restrictions on sharing commissions with unlicensed members?
- What language should be used instead of referral fee if needed?

### Ethics And Compliance

- What disclosures are required when talking to sellers?
- What is the policy for elderly, disabled, estate, probate, foreclosure, or hardship sellers?
- What is the policy when seller would likely net more through a retail listing?
- Who reviews questionable situations before an offer is made?
- Who confirms Georgia legal/compliance requirements around wholesaling, assignments, double closings, and agent involvement?

### New Member Process

- Is Chase being considered as a member, passive investor, tool-cost contributor, or outside collaborator?
- What survey questions must new members answer?
- Is unanimous approval required?
- Does a new member contribute capital, expertise, labor, or all three?
- Does new member approval affect ownership or voting rights?

## Suggested First 2 Week Trial Plan

### Week 1

- Onboard VA.
- Give tool access.
- Pick one primary market.
- Pick one primary strategy.
- Pull first list.
- Start SMS or mail workflow.
- Build tracker.
- Have VA produce daily reports.
- Review first deal briefs together.

### Week 2

- Increase outreach volume if week 1 tracking looks clean.
- Have VA research both land and residential samples.
- Test seller follow-up process.
- Test county verification process.
- Test deal voting process.
- Decide whether hours are being fully used.
- Decide whether to keep, reduce, or expand the role.

## Immediate Assignments Before VA Starts

- Finalize contractor agreement.
- Decide advance or no advance.
- Decide start date and exact working hours.
- Choose the first tool trial.
- Choose first target market.
- Choose first campaign type.
- Create VA-facing group chat.
- Keep internal strategy chat separate.
- Create lead tracker.
- Create deal brief template.
- Assign a daily manager for the VA.
- Assign backup manager.
- Confirm who approves offers.
- Confirm who handles contracts.
- Confirm who receives daily reports.

VA First Day Schedule
12:00 PM-6:00 PM

Hi everyone, here is the proposed first-day schedule for the VA tomorrow.

12:00-12:15 PM | Welcome + Setup
Quick welcome, introductions, and confirmation that she has the right access, tools, and context for the day.

12:15-2:00 PM | Deal Acquisition + Disposition Download with Sophie
Sophie will walk her through how the process usually works, including lead flow, acquisition steps, disposition, handoffs, key terminology, and where the VA fits into the process.

2:00-3:00 PM | Break
She can use this hour to take a break, review notes, and get settled before the afternoon session.

3:00-3:45 PM | Agreement Signing + Expectations with Aaliyah
Aaliyah will go over the agreement, role expectations, communication standards, confidentiality, schedule, and what success should look like in the first few weeks.

3:45-4:30 PM | Initial Investment Strategy
Aaliyah will explain our initial investment strategy, target deals, deal criteria, red flags, priorities, and when items should be escalated.

4:30-5:30 PM | Admin Portal Walkthrough
We will introduce the admin portal built for her day-to-day workflow, including tasks, lead/deal tracking, status updates, notes, and daily expectations.

5:30-6:00 PM | Practice + Q&A
She can walk through a sample task or sample lead in the portal, ask questions, and leave with clear priorities for day two.

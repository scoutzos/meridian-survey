VA First Day Schedule
12:00 PM-6:00 PM

Hi everyone, here is the proposed first-day schedule for the VA today.

Google Meet: https://meet.google.com/vqb-jdbe-ssc

12:00-12:15 PM | Agreement Signing + Welcome
Begin with the agreement signing, then a quick welcome, introductions, and confirmation that she has the right access, tools, and context for the day.

12:15-2:00 PM | Deal Acquisition + Disposition Download with Sophie
Sophie will walk her through how the process usually works, including lead flow, acquisition steps, disposition, handoffs, key terminology, and where the VA fits into the process.

2:00-3:00 PM | Break
She can use this hour to take a break, review notes, and get settled before the afternoon session.

3:00-3:45 PM | Initial Investment Strategy
Walk through the initial investment strategy, target deals, deal criteria, red flags, priorities, and when items should be escalated.

3:45-4:30 PM | Admin Portal Walkthrough
Introduce the admin portal built for her day-to-day workflow, including tasks, lead/deal tracking, status updates, notes, and daily expectations.

4:30-5:30 PM | Guided Practice Task
Walk through a sample task or sample lead in the portal so she can practice the workflow before closing the day.

5:30-6:00 PM | Expectations + Q&A
Close with role expectations, communication standards, success markers, questions, and clear priorities for day two.

VA First Day Schedule
12:00 PM-6:00 PM

Hi everyone, here is the proposed first-day schedule for the VA today.

Google Meet: https://meet.google.com/vqb-jdbe-ssc

12:00-12:30 PM | Agreement Signing + Welcome
Begin with the agreement signing, then a quick welcome, introductions, role basics, and the flow for the day.

12:30-1:15 PM | Access + Tool Setup
Confirm Google Meet, WhatsApp/group chat, admin portal, CRM or deal tracker, shared docs, email, calendar, and any needed logins.

1:15-2:00 PM | Admin Portal Orientation
Give her a high-level tour of where daily work lives: tasks, leads, notes, statuses, follow-ups, and where to ask questions.

2:00-3:00 PM | Break
She can use this hour to take a break, review notes, and get settled before the afternoon session.

3:00-4:30 PM | Deal Acquisition + Disposition Workflow with Sophie
Sophie will walk through lead intake, acquisition, disposition, handoffs, key terminology, and one sample deal from start to finish.

4:30-5:15 PM | Initial Investment Strategy
Walk through target deals, deal criteria, red flags, priority markets, and when something should be escalated.

5:15-5:45 PM | Guided Practice Task
Have her enter or update a sample lead or deal in the portal based on Sophie's walkthrough.

5:45-6:00 PM | Expectations + Q&A
Close with communication expectations, SLA basics, day-two priorities, and any final questions.

# Meridian Member And VA Onboarding Guide

Use this guide for the first week of operating in Meridian after the rebuild.

## Plain-English Map

Meridian has one flow:

```text
Land list -> seller outreach -> interested lead -> deal packet -> member review -> disposition -> project/closing
```

The VA works the front of the pipeline. Members review, decide, fund, approve, and help unblock work.

## VA First Day

1. Log in and confirm you land on `VA Desk`.
2. Clock in or start your shift.
3. Open `Imported Lists` and confirm the current list is visible.
4. Work leads from the seller queue.
5. Use the lead search before creating anything new.
6. Send texts only to eligible numbers.
7. Record the seller outcome after every meaningful touch.
8. Convert interested sellers into a deal brief when there is enough information for members to review.
9. Complete assigned VA tasks from the Today queue.
10. End the shift by submitting the daily brief.

## VA Daily Brief Expectations

At the end of each shift, the VA brief should answer:

- What lists were worked?
- How many texts, calls, replies, and follow-ups happened?
- Which sellers showed interest?
- Which deal briefs were submitted?
- Which member-assigned tasks were completed?
- What is blocked or needs a member decision?
- What should happen next shift?

Completed VA tasks should be marked done before the brief is submitted so the daily brief count is accurate.

## Member Daily Routine

1. Start at `Home`.
2. Open `Tasks` for votes, assigned work, VA tasks, money items, and surveys.
3. Open `Deal Reviews` for packets needing member review.
4. Open `CRM` to inspect seller/buyer records, campaigns, messages, and disposition.
5. Open `Operations` for VA briefs, blocked VA work, time review, money ops, and escalations.
6. Open `Money` for balances, capital calls, expenses, and planning.
7. Open `Projects` for approved deals that moved into execution.

## Member-To-VA Task Rules

Use `Tasks` when a member needs the VA to do something.

Good VA tasks:

- Call this seller tomorrow.
- Find missing parcel or county information.
- Pull comps for this deal.
- Verify whether this imported lead is a duplicate.
- Follow up on a seller reply.
- Research zoning, access, utilities, or taxes.

Every VA task should have:

- A clear title.
- A due date when possible.
- A linked record when possible.
- Enough context that the VA can complete it without asking what it means.

If the VA marks a task blocked, members should reply in Operations or the task detail so the VA can continue.

## CRM Rules

Use CRM for working records, not just viewing them.

- `Inbox`: seller replies and unmatched SMS.
- `Deals`: deal packet queue and CRM deal context.
- `Buyers`: buyer records, buy boxes, proof of funds, and offer history.
- `Dispo`: campaigns, buyer outreach, offers, member offer decisions, and closing handoff.
- `Records`: contacts and property records.

CRM should always connect back to the shared Opportunity File or deal packet.

## Disposition Rules

When a buyer offer comes in:

1. Record the buyer offer in CRM Dispo.
2. Members receive decision tasks.
3. Members decide accept, counter, reject, or withdrawn.
4. Accepted offers create project/closing handoff tasks.
5. Rejected or withdrawn offers create follow-up tasks so the campaign does not silently die.

## What Goes Where

| Need | Go To |
| --- | --- |
| Daily priorities | Home |
| Assigned work and votes | Tasks |
| Deal packet review | Deal Reviews or Opportunity File |
| Seller/buyer communication | CRM |
| VA daily brief and blocked work | Operations |
| Money, capital calls, reimbursements | Money |
| Active execution after approval | Projects |
| Final files and references | Documents |
| Meeting notes and transcripts | Meetings |
| Official decisions and vote record | Decisions |
| Archive, older resources, member profiles | Hub |

## Launch Week Rules

- Do not create side spreadsheets for work that belongs in Meridian.
- Do not assign VA work by text without also creating a task.
- Do not decide on a buyer offer without updating CRM Dispo.
- Do not treat a deal as approved unless the vote/agreement/project flow supports it.
- If something feels confusing, write down the exact page and action so it can be fixed.

## Escalation Path

Use this order:

1. VA blocks a task or notes the issue in the daily brief.
2. Member responds in Operations or the task detail.
3. If it affects a deal, update the Opportunity File or CRM record.
4. If it affects money or approval, route through Tasks, Decisions, or Money.
5. If the system is wrong, document it in the QA runbook and fix it before launch.
